LeadSeed

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026

LeadSeed is a productivity and sales management tool that operates, among other environments, as a Chrome extension with a side panel. It allows users to manage contacts and leads, tasks, scheduling, communications, and collaboration features. This Privacy Policy explains what information LeadSeed may process, how it is used, and which services may receive it when necessary to provide requested features.

Limited Use principle: LeadSeed uses data obtained through extension permissions and connected APIs only to provide or improve user-facing features requested by the user. LeadSeed does not sell personal data, does not use it for personalized advertising, does not provide it to data brokers, and does not use it to determine creditworthiness or financial eligibility.

1. Information LeadSeed May Process

Account and authentication information. We may process email address, account identifiers, basic profile information provided by the authentication provider, and session tokens required to keep the user signed in.

Contact and lead data. LeadSeed may store information entered or imported by the user, such as name, phone number, email address, company, notes, sales status, and other fields related to contact management.

Tasks, scheduling, and user-created content. We may process tasks, notes, availability, appointments, participants, message templates, email content prepared for sending, internal conversations, and collaboration content created within LeadSeed.

Local preferences. The extension may store browser preferences, interface settings, and other local configuration required to remember the state of the application.

2. How We Use Information

  • Authenticate users and maintain active sessions.
  • Display, organize, update, import, and export contacts and leads.
  • Manage tasks, schedules, availability, appointments, and reminders.
  • Prepare and send communications initiated by the user.
  • Synchronize Google features authorized by the user.
  • Provide chat, community, and collaboration features available within LeadSeed.
  • Maintain the security, continuity, and technical operation of the service.

3. Google Sign-In, Gmail, and Google Calendar

When a user connects a Google Account, LeadSeed may receive basic identity information, including the account email address, together with OAuth tokens and the scopes granted by the user. LeadSeed may store the access token, refresh token, granted scopes, and token expiration information in order to maintain the authorized integration.

Gmail. LeadSeed uses the Gmail API to send messages that the user chooses to send from the application. LeadSeed's current implementation uses Gmail sending access and does not require reading the user's inbox or the content of received emails for this functionality.

Google Calendar. When authorized, LeadSeed may create, update, and delete calendar events; add attendees; generate Google Meet links; and check availability through Google Calendar Free/Busy functionality. When creating an appointment, the event may include information the user has associated with a lead, such as name, phone number, email address, company, notes, and attendees.

To synchronize availability, LeadSeed may query occupied time ranges from Google Calendar and store the time blocks necessary to help prevent double-booking. This functionality does not require copying the full content of every calendar event.

Google API Services User Data Policy.
The use of information received from Google Workspace scopes will adhere to the Google User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

LeadSeed does not use information obtained through Google Workspace APIs to develop, improve, or train generalized or non-personalized artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

4. Third-Party Services

LeadSeed may use external service providers only when necessary to provide requested functionality. These may include:

  • Supabase: authentication, database, synchronization, and backend functions.
  • Google: authentication, Gmail API, Google Calendar API, and authorized OAuth services.
  • Resend: email processing and delivery when the user chooses to use a Resend channel.
  • EmailJS: a legacy email integration available when the user chooses to use it.
  • WhatsApp Web: LeadSeed may open conversations or links to WhatsApp Web to facilitate communications initiated by the user.

These providers receive only the information reasonably necessary to provide the corresponding functionality and are subject to their own terms and privacy policies.

5. Email Service Credentials

When a user configures credentials for a supported email channel, LeadSeed processes them to enable that channel. The backend implementation encrypts credentials for supported channels before storing them in the database. These credentials are not used for advertising purposes and are not sold to third parties.

6. Security

LeadSeed uses HTTPS/TLS connections to protect information while it is transmitted and applies authentication and authorization controls designed to limit access to user data. No system can guarantee absolute security, so we maintain reasonable safeguards intended to reduce unauthorized access, use, or disclosure.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Data is retained while it is needed to provide LeadSeed, while the account remains active, or until the user deletes information available within the application. Google OAuth tokens are retained only while necessary to maintain the authorized integration. Users may also revoke LeadSeed's access through their Google Account security settings.

Certain technical records or backups may be retained for a limited period when necessary for security, service continuity, fraud prevention, incident resolution, or legal obligations.

8. Human Access to User Data

LeadSeed personnel or collaborators do not access a user's private content unless it is strictly necessary to provide support requested by the user, investigate a security or abuse incident, comply with a legal obligation, or perform internal operations using aggregated or anonymized data.

9. Sale, Advertising, and Other Prohibited Uses

LeadSeed does not use data obtained through the extension or Google Workspace APIs to:

  • sell or rent personal information;
  • provide personalized advertising or retargeting;
  • create commercial profiles unrelated to user-requested functionality;
  • determine creditworthiness, credit, insurance, or financial eligibility;
  • transfer data to data brokers;
  • train generalized AI/ML models.

10. User Rights and Controls

Depending on the available features, users may access, correct, export, or delete contact information and other records they manage in LeadSeed. Users may also revoke Google permissions from their Google Account. For privacy questions, data access requests, correction requests, export requests, or deletion requests, contact hentimes@gmail.com.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when LeadSeed features, service providers, or applicable requirements change. The date of the most recent update will appear at the top of this page. If a change materially modifies how Google data or other sensitive data is used, we will update the relevant disclosures before using the information for the new purpose.

12. Contact

For questions regarding this Privacy Policy, LeadSeed's handling of personal data, or requests related to your information, contact hentimes@gmail.com.
